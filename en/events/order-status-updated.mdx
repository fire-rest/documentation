`order.status_updated` is an order's **tracking notice**: it fires every time something happens to the order, in three lanes.

| Lane (`source`) | What it reports | Statuses |
|---|---|---|
| `kds` | The kitchen | `preparing`, `ready`, `dispatched`, `cancelled` |
| `order` | The order | `opened`, `corrected`, `completed`, `force_closed`, `cancelled` |
| `fiscal` | The fiscal document | `numbered`, `rejected`, `invoiced`, `cancellation_failed`, `reversed` |

It's a **lightweight notice**: it carries the order's identity and the change, **not** the full order. If you need the detail (lines, payments, taxes), read the order again with [`GET /api/v1/external/orders/{orderId}`](/en/api-reference/get-order).

## Two kinds of event: work and tracking

| | `order.opened` · `order.completed` · `order.invoiced` · `order.reversed` · `order.cancelled` | `order.status_updated` |
|---|---|---|
| What it's for | Doing **work** with the order: preparing it, invoicing it, injecting it into your system | **Following** the order: knowing what happened to it |
| What it carries | The full order | The order's identity and the change |
| When to use it | Your system has to act on the order's content | You want to show or record the order's journey |

If you only need to know **where the order is**, subscribe to `order.status_updated` and pick the statuses you need.

## Each flow picks which statuses it receives

In the flow's trigger you pick the statuses you want, grouped into **Kitchen**, **Order** and **Invoice**. The flow receives only those.

<Note>
  **If you don't pick anything**, the flow receives `preparing`, `ready` and `dispatched` from the kitchen — what this event always sent. Flows that already existed stay the same. The kitchen cancellation (`kds` / `cancelled`) is **not** part of that default: if you want it, check it.
</Note>

## When it fires

| Lane | Status | When it fires |
|---|---|---|
| `kds` | `preparing` · `ready` · `dispatched` | The KDS reports a kitchen advance that passes the anti-regression gate (it never goes back). |
| `kds` | `cancelled` | The KDS **confirms the kitchen cancelled** the order. Different from `order` / `cancelled`, which is the order's cancellation. |
| `order` | `opened` | The order was created open (unpaid). |
| `order` | `corrected` | An open order was corrected with [`PUT /api/v1/external/orders/{orderId}`](/en/api-reference/update-order). Can happen several times. |
| `order` | `completed` | The order was created paid, or an open order was paid. |
| `order` | `force_closed` | The order was still open when the store's business day closed, and it was closed automatically. |
| `order` | `cancelled` | The order was cancelled. |
| `fiscal` | `numbered` | The order received its fiscal numbering (at creation, at payment or later). |
| `fiscal` | `rejected` | The tax authority rejected the document. Can repeat (retries with another reason). |
| `fiscal` | `invoiced` | The tax authority authorized the document. |
| `fiscal` | `cancellation_failed` | The authority rejected the void: the document **is still valid**. |
| `fiscal` | `reversed` | The authority confirmed the document's void. |

Notices from the `order` and `fiscal` lanes, and the kitchen cancellation, are **delivered even if the order is cancelled later**: they're facts that already happened.

| | |
|---|---|
| Coverage | **All countries** |
| Idempotency key | `data.orderId` + `data.source` + the lane's change: `kitchen.status`, `lifecycle.event` + `lifecycle.revision`, or `fiscal.sequence`. Not `event.emittedAt`: two notices of the same order (for example `order` / `completed` and `fiscal` / `numbered`) can be born at the same instant. See [Idempotency on your side](/en/events/delivery#idempotency-on-your-side) |
| Fires more than once | Yes: once per change in each lane your flow picked |

## Ordering the notices

**Webhooks don't arrive in order.** Each notice carries what you need to discard a stale one, **within its lane**:

- **Kitchen** (`kitchen.rank`) and **order** (`lifecycle.rank`) only move forward. Keep the highest `rank` you've seen per lane and discard a lower one.
  - Kitchen: `preparing` 1 · `ready` 2 · `dispatched` 3 · `cancelled` 4.
  - Order: `opened` 1 · `corrected` 2 · `completed` 3 · `force_closed` 4 · `cancelled` 5. Between two corrections, the highest `revision` wins.
- **Fiscal** can genuinely go back: a rejected void returns the document to authorized, and a rejection can be retried and end up authorized. That's why it has no `rank` but `fiscal.sequence`, a counter of the order's fiscal changes that **always goes up**, even when the status goes back. Keep the highest `sequence` and discard a lower one. The number can skip values: some fiscal changes don't produce a notice.

Don't compare one lane's `rank` with another's: they're different scales.

## What's in `data`

Each notice carries the order's **identity**, the `source` field and **only its lane's block**. The other two are `null`.

### Identity and context

Same as v1: `orderId`, `orderCode`, `externalOrderId`, `businessDayDate`, `status` (the order's business status: `OPEN`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED` or `FORCE_CLOSED`), `channel` (`code`, `uid`), `store` (`code`, `name`, `account`, `vendor`) and `fulfillment.service.code`.

<ResponseField name="source" type="string">
  **New in v2.** Which lane the notice belongs to: `kds`, `order` or `fiscal`. It tells you which block has data; the other two arrive as `null`.
</ResponseField>

### The `kitchen` block (`kds` lane)

Unchanged from v1. It's only present when `source = "kds"`; in the other lanes it's `null`. `kitchen.status` can be `preparing`, `ready`, `dispatched` or `cancelled` (`rank` 1 to 4).

### The `lifecycle` block (`order` lane)

<ResponseField name="lifecycle" type="object | null">
  **New in v2.** Only present when `source = "order"`.

  <Expandable title="lifecycle">
    <ResponseField name="event" type="string">
      `opened` | `corrected` | `completed` | `force_closed` | `cancelled`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="rank" type="number">
      `opened` 1 · `corrected` 2 · `completed` 3 · `force_closed` 4 · `cancelled` 5. Only grows: discard a notice with a `rank` lower than the last one you applied.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="previousEvent" type="string | null">
      The state the order was in before: `opened` or `completed`; `null` when the order is born with this movement or it is not known. It is not the previous notice — a corrected order still reports `opened`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="occurredAt" type="string">
      ISO 8601 — when it happened, according to Fire.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="revision" type="number | null">
      Only on `corrected`: the order version the correction left. Between two corrections, the highest wins.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reason" type="string | null">
      Only on `force_closed`: why it stayed open — `ABANDONED` (no payment), `PAYMENT_FAILED` (payment rejected), `PAYMENT_PENDING` (payment not completed), `PARTIAL_PAYMENT` (paid less than the total) or `INTEGRATION_ERROR` (any other cause). `null` on the other movements.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

### The `fiscal` block (`fiscal` lane)

<ResponseField name="fiscal" type="object | null">
  **New in v2.** Only present when `source = "fiscal"`. It has no `rank`: see [Ordering the notices](#ordering-the-notices).

  <Expandable title="fiscal">
    <ResponseField name="event" type="string">
      `numbered` | `rejected` | `invoiced` | `cancellation_failed` | `reversed`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="sequence" type="number">
      Counter of the order's fiscal changes. Always goes up, even when the status goes back. Discard a notice with a `sequence` lower than the last one you applied.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string | null">
      The fiscal status **left** on the order after this change (for example `authorized`, `rejected`, `cancelled`).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="previousStatus" type="string | null">
      The previous fiscal status.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="documentNumber" type="string | null">
      The fiscal document's number, if it already has one.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="countryCode" type="string | null">
      The document's country (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="occurredAt" type="string">
      ISO 8601 — when it happened.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="callbackId" type="string | null">
      Id of the fiscal provider's notice that produced the change, if it came from one.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>

## What changes from v1

| | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| What it reports | Kitchen only | Kitchen, order and invoice |
| Who picks | You received everything | Each flow picks the statuses; with no selection, the usual |
| New fields in `data` | — | `source`, `lifecycle`, `fiscal` |
| `kitchen` block | Always | Same as before on kitchen notices; `null` in the other lanes |
| Kitchen cancellation | Documented but not emitted | Emitted (`kds` / `cancelled`), only to flows that pick it |

For a flow that already existed and didn't pick statuses, the only visible change is the three new fields in `data` (`source: "kds"`, `lifecycle: null`, `fiscal: null`). If your receiver validates the body with a strict schema that rejects unknown fields, update it to accept them.

## Typical use cases

- **Order tracking for the customer** — show "preparing", "ready", "invoiced" or "cancelled" in an app or a screen.
- **Notify the aggregator** — tell iFood, Rappi or 99Food the order is ready or was cancelled in the kitchen.
- **Fiscal reconciliation** — know, without reading the full order, when the document was numbered, authorized, rejected or voided.
- **Abandoned orders** — spot the ones closed automatically at the end of the day (`force_closed`) and why.

## What it does NOT do

- **It doesn't carry the full order.** That's what the work events or reading the order are for.
- **It doesn't replace `order.completed`, `order.invoiced` or `order.reversed`.** Those ask for work; this one reports what happened.
- **It doesn't send you what you didn't pick.** A flow with no selection receives only the kitchen advance.
