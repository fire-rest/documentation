---
title: "Business day close"
api: "GET https://app.fire.rest/api/v2/external/stores/{storeId}/business-days/{date}"
description: "Read a store's closed business day: payments by method, processor and card brand, and the cash count of every shift."
authMethod: "key"
---

<Warning>
  **Partner API.** This endpoint is intended for platform integrators. Standard Fire customers do not have direct access — contact your account manager if you need this integration.
</Warning>

Returns one closed business day of a store: **how it was paid** — grouped by payment method, then processor, then card brand, with the cash handed over and the change — and **how the cash drawer balanced** in every shift: opening float, cash sales, withdrawals, counted cash and the difference.

The [`store.business_day_closed`](/en/events/store-day-closed) event is lean on purpose: it tells you which day closed, when and who closed it. This endpoint is where you read the detail, with the store and the date.

## Authentication

<ParamField header="x-api-key" type="string" required>
  Your Fire API key with the `business-days:read` scope. The key **must be vendor-scoped** (account + vendor binding) — keys without a `vendorId` are rejected with `403`.

  The store is looked up inside your key's account and vendor. A store that belongs to someone else returns `404`, exactly like one that does not exist.
</ParamField>

## Parameters

<ParamField path="storeId" type="string" required>
  The store's UUID in Fire **or** its store code (`K000`, not case-sensitive). The code is unambiguous because the API key pins the vendor; if two stores of your vendor share a code, the response is `409 AMBIGUOUS_STORE_CODE` and you use the UUID.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="date" type="string" required>
  Business day, `YYYY-MM-DD`, in the store's local calendar.
</ParamField>

## Request

<RequestExample>
```http By store code
GET https://app.fire.rest/api/v2/external/stores/K000/business-days/2026-11-10
x-api-key: <your_api_key>
```
</RequestExample>

## How to read the response

- **`summary.expectedCashSales` and the shifts do not have to add up.** The day's figure is the cash charged in the day; each shift's is the cash Fire attributed to that cashier and time window. Cashiers who did not close their shift, or a shift reported without a cashier, make them differ.
- **Read `declaredCash` with `apiVersion`.** A v2 shift counts the whole drawer, float included; a v1 shift counts net of the float. `discrepancy` means the same thing in both.
- **A missing card brand is not hidden.** If your POS sends `card.brand` empty, those payments show under `brand: null`.

## Days closed before this contract

What a close did not store, it does not show later. Fire does **not** rebuild it from the orders.

- Days grouped only by processor come back with `processors: null`, and `method` holds the code they were grouped by.
- Days closed before card brands and cash handed over were recorded come back with `brands: null` and `cash: null`.
- v1 shifts come back with `withdrawalsTotal: 0`, and `openingBalance` only when the POS sent it (informational).
- Days without a cash summary come back with `unreconciledOperators: null`.

## Errors

| HTTP | `code` | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | — | Empty `storeId`, or `date` is not a real `YYYY-MM-DD` date |
| `401` | — | Invalid, expired or revoked API key |
| `403` | — | Key without `business-days:read`, or not vendor-scoped |
| `404` | — | The store is not in your key's vendor |
| `404` | `BUSINESS_DAY_NOT_FOUND` | The day has no close |
| `409` | `AMBIGUOUS_STORE_CODE` | Two stores of your vendor share that code: use the UUID |
| `409` | `BUSINESS_DAY_OPEN` | The day is still open. Fire does not return partial data: ask again after the close |
