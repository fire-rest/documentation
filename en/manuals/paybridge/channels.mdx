---
title: "PayBridge channels"
description: "How to choose which sales channels PayBridge considers (Super Admin)."
---

PayBridge works on top of a catalog of **sales channels** (POS, Kiosk, Web, App, and others). As a Super Admin you decide **which of those channels PayBridge considers**. The channels you enable here are the ones that appear on the account-level availability and configuration screens.

## Prerequisites

- Backoffice access at **[app.fire.rest](https://app.fire.rest)**, with the **Super Admin** role.

## 1. Open Channels

In the Super Admin sidebar, open **PayBridge → Channels**. You'll see the master channel catalog with a switch for each one.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/channels/01-channels-list.png" alt="Channel catalog with enable switches" />
</Frame>

## 2. Enable or disable a channel

Use each row's switch to indicate whether PayBridge considers that channel. The change applies instantly.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/channels/02-toggle-channel.png" alt="A channel switch being turned on" />
</Frame>

<Note>
If you don't enable any channel, PayBridge uses a default set: **POS, Kiosk, Web, and App**.
</Note>

## 3. Filter the list

You can filter by status (for example, show only the **Active** ones) to quickly review which channels are considered.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/channels/03-filter-active.png" alt="Channel list filtered by active" />
</Frame>

## How this is used at the account level

The active channels appear as editable options in [Method availability](/en/manuals/paybridge/availability): when you turn a method on in a store you can choose which channels it operates in. A device's channel (POS or Kiosk) is implicit from its type.
