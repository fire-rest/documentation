---
title: "Create or edit a payment method"
description: "How to add and maintain PayBridge's global payment method catalog (Super Admin)."
---

The **payment method catalog** is global: it defines which methods exist in PayBridge, in which
countries they can be offered, and how each one is processed. It's a **Super Admin** task; which
method is on in each store is decided later in
[Availability](/en/manuals/paybridge/availability).

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Fire backoffice at **[app.fire.rest](https://app.fire.rest)**, with the **Super Admin** role.
- For DSI-routed methods: the **provider** and the **payment method** as named by the Paybridge DSI
  catalog, plus the country's [DSI connection](/en/manuals/paybridge/dsi-connections).

## 1. Open the catalog

In the Super Admin sidebar, open **PayBridge → Payment methods**. The table shows one row per method
with its **Type**, **Routing**, **Countries**, **Card brands**, and **Status**, and you can filter by
each of those fields.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/01-catalog-list.png" alt="PayBridge payment method catalog" />
</Frame>

## 2. Add a method

Click **Add payment method**. To change an existing one, use **Edit** in its row.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/02-add-button.png" alt="Add payment method button" />
</Frame>

The form takes up the **whole screen** and is organized in four tabs: **General**, **Integration**,
**Connection**, and **General credentials**. You can walk through them with **Previous** and **Next**,
or click a tab directly. **Cancel** and **Save** sit at the top right, next to the title.

<Note>
If something is missing when you save, the tab with problems gets a **red dot** and the form jumps to
the first tab with errors.
</Note>

## 3. General tab

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/03-tab-general.png" alt="General tab of the payment method editor" />
</Frame>

- **Name** — what the method is called (for example *Deuna / Banco Pichincha*).
- **Code** — the method's identifier. It is **auto-generated** from the name and you can adjust it
  while creating; once created it **can't be changed**. It's the same code a channel sends as
  `methodCode` when [charging](/en/api-reference/paybridge-charge).
- **Description** — free reference text.
- **Logo URL** — square image (64×64 px, PNG or SVG). The preview shows next to it.
- **Available countries** — where the method can be offered. Pick **at least one**; a method can cover
  several.
- **Status** — the switch that makes the method available to accounts. Inactive hides it, but existing
  configurations **are kept**.

## 4. Integration tab

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/04-tab-integration.png" alt="Integration tab with processor routing" />
</Frame>

- **Processor routing** — who talks to the provider when charging. Today only **DSI** is available:
  *Fire native* and *Local bridge* show up with the **Coming soon** tag.
- **Payment flow** — you don't choose it: it's **derived** from the routing. With DSI the flow is
  always a **payment link (redirect)**.
- **Card brands** — the brands the method supports (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover…). These are the
  ones shown to the customer later.

## 5. Connection tab

This tab depends on the routing. With **DSI** you'll see three blocks:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/05-tab-connection.png" alt="Connection tab with the DSI configuration" />
</Frame>

- **DSI config** — the **DSI provider** (for example `deuna`, `mach`, `rutpay`) and the **DSI payment
  method** (the `paymentMethod` field value, for example `DEUNA-BANCO-PICHINCHA`). Both come from the
  provider catalog: `GET /api/paybridge/v1/payments/providers`.
- **DSI connection per country** — one row per country selected in General, with its base URL, status,
  and last test. From here you can **configure and test it without leaving the editor**; it's the same
  global connection that lives in [DSI connections](/en/manuals/paybridge/dsi-connections), shared by
  every DSI method.
- **Endpoints** — they come **prefilled** with the Paybridge DSI paths and are resolved at runtime
  against the country's base URL.

<Note>
If the chosen routing doesn't need to talk to an external provider, the tab shows a **No connection
required** notice and there's nothing to fill in.
</Note>

## 6. General credentials tab

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/06-tab-credentials.png" alt="General credentials tab with the method fields" />
</Frame>

Here you declare the keys and values **at method level**, the same for every account. For each field
you define **Key**, **Label**, **Scope level**, **Validation (regex)**, **Help text**, and whether it
is **Required**.

<Info>
Anything specific to a brand, store, or device **doesn't go here**: it's loaded in each account's
[Configuration per level](/en/manuals/paybridge/configuration).
</Info>

## 7. Save

When creating, the button is **Create payment method**; when editing, **Save changes**. Fire confirms
with a toast and goes back to the catalog.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/07-create-button.png" alt="Create payment method button" />
</Frame>

## Activate or deactivate a method

You don't need to open the editor: the actions menu of each catalog row has **Activate** and
**Deactivate**, with a confirmation. Deactivating hides the method from accounts without deleting
anything.
