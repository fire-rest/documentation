---
title: "Create a payment method"
description: "How to add and configure a payment method in the PayBridge catalog from the backoffice (Super Admin)."
---

This guide covers how to create a payment method in the **PayBridge** catalog. The catalog is global: the methods you create here become available to assign to accounts. This is a **Super Admin** task.

## Prerequisites

- Backoffice access with the **Super Admin** role.
- Knowing which routing will process the method (today, **DSI**).
- The processor connection details per country (you can load them during creation or later, in [DSI connections](/en/manuals/paybridge/dsi-connections)).

## 1. Open the methods catalog

In the Super Admin sidebar, open the **PayBridge → Payment methods** collapsible. You'll see the list of existing methods with their status, countries, and routing.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/01-catalog-list.png" alt="PayBridge payment methods catalog list" />
</Frame>

## 2. Create a new method

In the top-right corner click **Add payment method**. The guided editor opens, organized in four steps.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/02-add-button.png" alt="Add method button in the catalog" />
</Frame>

## 3. General step

Define the method's identity:

- **Name** — the display name (editable at any time).
- **Code** — the method's unique identifier. It's **auto-generated** from the name and **cannot be changed** after saving.
- **Description** *(optional)* — supporting text for the method.
- **Logo URL** *(optional)* — square image recommended (64×64 px, PNG or SVG).
- **Available countries** — one or more of the available countries (Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Venezuela). Pick at least one.
- **Status** — the switch that indicates whether the method is available to assign to accounts.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/03-tab-general.png" alt="Editor General step: identity, countries, and status" />
</Frame>

Click **Next** to continue. If a required field is missing, an error dot appears on the corresponding step.

## 4. Integration step

Choose the **processor routing** that will process the method. Today the active routing is **DSI**; **Fire native** and **Local bridge** appear with a **Coming soon** badge. The **payment flow** is derived automatically from the routing (with DSI, it's always a payment link / redirect). Below, you can mark the **card brands** the method supports (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/04-tab-integration.png" alt="Integration step with routing cards (DSI active)" />
</Frame>

## 5. Connection step

When you choose DSI, the **DSI config** is enabled: enter the **DSI provider** (for example `deuna`, `mach`, `rutpay`) and the **DSI payment method** (the value of the `paymentMethod` field in the DSI API). Below, the **DSI connection per country** section lets you define/test the connection (**base URL** and credentials) for each country you selected on the General tab. The provider's **Endpoints** (create payment, check status, cancel, refund) come prefilled and are resolved at runtime against the country's base URL.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/05-tab-connection.png" alt="Connection step with DSI config, per-country connection, and endpoints" />
</Frame>

<Note>
Connection details are **global per country**. You can also manage them from the dedicated [DSI connections](/en/manuals/paybridge/dsi-connections) screen.
</Note>

## 6. General credentials step

Here you define the keys and values **at the method level**, the same for every account (for example, your company's general credentials with the processor). Anything specific to a brand, store, or device is **not** set here, but from the account screens ([Configuration by level](/en/manuals/paybridge/configuration)).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/06-tab-credentials.png" alt="General credentials step: key/value fields at the method level" />
</Frame>

## 7. Create the method

Click **Create payment method**. The method appears in the catalog (step 1) with the status you assigned. If you left it active, it's already visible at the account level in the selected countries.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/paybridge/payment-methods/07-create-button.png" alt="Create payment method button" />
</Frame>

## Edit a method

From the list, use the row's actions menu to **Edit**. Remember the **code** is immutable; every other field can be updated.
