title: "Terminal administration"
description: "Monitor the store's POS terminals, pause or retire a device, and close a cashier's register when the POS can't."
---

A terminal that goes wrong —lost, broken, or unlinked with the register still open— doesn't just stop selling: it can leave a cashier stuck, unable to open another register, and a register with cash nobody counted. This screen is where the manager fixes that from the backoffice, without touching the device.

To register a new terminal, see [Linking](/en/manuals/pos/linking).

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - **Suspend** is reversible. **Revoke** and **Delete** are not: the device has to be linked again.
  - If the terminal has an open register, **close it manually with the count before revoking or deleting it**. Revoking closes it without counting the cash.
  - **Close register manually** uses the same calculation as the POS and doesn't ask for authorization codes: whoever closes from the backoffice is already a manager.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Fire backoffice at **[app.fire.rest](https://app.fire.rest)**, with permissions on the store.
- The right country, vendor and store selected in the header.

## Available actions

| Action | When to use it | What happens on the POS |
|--------|----------------|-------------------------|
| **View details** | Audit the history and last activity | Nothing: it's read-only |
| **Suspend** | Pause the terminal for maintenance or a review | It stops operating; the register and shift stay intact |
| **Reactivate** | Enable a suspended terminal again | It operates again with the same register and shift |
| **Revoke** | Device lost, stolen or retired | Closes the register and shift without a count; it has to be linked again |
| **Delete** | Remove the terminal from the list | Same as revoke |
| **Close register manually** | The POS can't close the register | Closes the register with the count and ends the cashier's shift |

## 1. Open Terminals

In the top-left corner of the backoffice, open the channel selector and choose **Channel - POS**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/terminal-admin/01-backoffice-pos-channel.png" alt="Channel selector open with Channel - POS selected" />
</Frame>

In the side menu, open **Fire POS → Terminals**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/terminal-admin/02-backoffice-terminals-menu.png" alt="Fire POS side menu with Terminals selected" />
</Frame>

The page groups terminals in tabs:

- **Active**: linked, operating terminals.
- **Pending slots**: linking codes that haven't been used yet.
- **Suspended**: terminals paused by a manager.
- **Revoked**: retired terminals.

To also see deleted ones, check **Show deleted**.

---

## 2. Review each terminal's card

Each card shows the terminal name, its status, the store and how long ago it was last seen. A green dot means the device is online.

If a cashier has an open register, the card also shows who it is, since when it's open, the shift's sales, the **System cash**, the orders, and the **Close register manually** button.

Open the card's **⋯** menu to see the actions: **View details**, **Suspend**, **Revoke** and **Delete**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/terminal-admin/03-terminals-list-actions.png" alt="Card of a terminal with an open register and the actions menu expanded" />
</Frame>

<Info>
  The card's **System cash** comes from the register's record on the POS. When closing, the expected amount comes from Fire, which subtracts cancellations. That's why the two numbers can differ: the one that counts for the close is the dialog's.
</Info>

## 3. Check the details

**View details** opens the terminal's operational information:

- **Last activity**: the last time the device talked to Fire.
- **Other terminal data**: identifiers another system needs. In Ecuador, also the register's fiscal identifier.
- **Timeline**: registration, suspensions, reactivations and other status changes, with their date.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/terminal-admin/04-terminal-detail-timeline.png" alt="Terminal detail with the last activity and the timeline" />
</Frame>

Check this view before suspending or revoking a device: it tells you what happened to it and when.

## Close register manually

Use it when the POS can't close the register: the device was unlinked with the register open, it broke, or the cashier is gone. While the register stays open, that cashier can't sign in to another register or open a new one.

The close does the same as the POS one: it calculates the expected amount with Fire, asks for the reason if cash is missing, ends the cashier's shift and reconciles the close. The difference is that it doesn't ask for authorization codes and that **it isn't blind**: you see the expected amount before typing what you counted.

1. Count the cash in the drawer.
2. On the terminal's card, tap **Close register manually**.
3. Review the calculation: **opening float + cash sales (Fire) − withdrawals = expected in drawer**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/pos/terminal-admin/05-admin-close-dialog.png" alt="Close register dialog with the opening float, cash sales, withdrawals and the expected amount" />
   </Frame>

4. In **Counted cash**, type the total as you counted it, without a thousands separator. Colombian and Chilean pesos take whole numbers; other currencies take up to 2 decimals. The formatted amount appears below, so you can confirm what you typed.
5. If the count is lower than expected, choose the **Shortage reason**. It's required: on the POS that reason comes with the manager's code; here you choose it.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/pos/terminal-admin/06-admin-close-shortage.png" alt="Close register dialog with a shortage and the list of reasons expanded" />
   </Frame>

6. In **Why it's being closed from the backoffice**, explain the situation in at least 10 characters. It stays in the close's audit trail.
7. Tap **Close register**.

**What happens next:**

- The register shows as **Forced close** in **Fire POS → Register closes**, with your user and the reason you wrote.
- The cashier's shift ends. If the POS is still on, it goes back to the sign-in screen.
- The close is reconciled with Fire the same way as one made on the POS. If that reconciliation fails, the register stays closed anyway and the notice tells you.

<Warning>
  **The close can't be undone.** Before tapping **Close register**, check the line with the formatted amount below the count: an extra or missing zero becomes the cashier's overage or shortage.
</Warning>

## Suspend a terminal

Use **Suspend** to pause a terminal temporarily and reversibly, for example during maintenance.

1. In the terminal's **⋯** menu, choose **Suspend**.
2. Write the reason and confirm.

The POS stops operating and shows the cashier a notice. The register and the shift stay intact in the system.

## Reactivate a suspended terminal

Once the problem is solved, open the **Suspended** tab, choose **Reactivate** on the terminal and confirm.

The POS picks up the change on its next sync. The cashier operates again with the same register and shift, without linking the device again.

## Revoke or delete a terminal

Use **Revoke** or **Delete** when the device is retired for good: it was lost or stolen, fraud was detected, or the hardware was replaced.

1. If the card shows an open register, close it first with **Close register manually**.
2. In the **⋯** menu, choose **Revoke** or **Delete**.
3. Write the reason and confirm.

The device stops operating and loses its link. To use that hardware again, create a new slot and link it again: see [Linking](/en/manuals/pos/linking).

<Warning>
  **Revoking or deleting with the register open closes it without a count.** The register shows as **Forced close** with no declared cash and no reconciliation with Fire, and nobody knows whether cash was missing. Close the register manually with the count, and only then revoke the terminal.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A cashier can't open a register because it says one is already open">
    Their previous register stayed open on another terminal, or on the same one after unlinking it. Find that terminal in **Active**, tap **Close register manually** and close it with the count. Then the cashier can start a new shift.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The card shows different cash than the close dialog">
    The card uses the register's record on the POS; the dialog uses Fire's expected amount, which subtracts cancellations. The dialog's is the one that counts for the close.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Close register button doesn't enable">
    Check three things: that the expected amount loaded (if not, tap **Retry**), that the count has a valid format, and that the reason has at least 10 characters. If there's a shortage, you also have to choose the reason.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The terminal keeps operating after suspending it">
    The POS picks up the change on its next sync, which can take up to a minute. If it keeps operating, confirm you suspended the right terminal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I need to use a revoked terminal again">
    A revoked terminal can't be reactivated. Create a new slot in **Terminals → Create slot** and link the device again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see the Terminals option">
    Confirm you have **Channel - POS** selected and permissions on the store.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
