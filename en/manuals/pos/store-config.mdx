---
title: "Store configuration"
description: "Adjust how the POS runs in each store: service types, parallel orders, how long kiosk orders stay visible, locator by number or by name, upsell suggestions, on-screen keyboard and when to require the customer's details."
---

Not every store runs the same way: one has a drive-thru and another doesn't, one hands out buzzers and another calls the customer by name, and in some countries the law requires the customer's details above a certain amount. This screen adjusts the POS for each store without touching the terminals: the changes reach them on their own.

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - The configuration is **per store**: it applies to every terminal of the store selected in the header.
  - Each option has a **default value for the store's country**. Whatever you change is marked as **Custom**.
  - Changes reach the POS without restarting it, as soon as you tap **Save**.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Fire backoffice at **[app.fire.rest](https://app.fire.rest)**, with permissions on the store.
- **Channel - POS** and the store selected in the header.

## 1. Open Store config

In the side menu, open **Fire POS → Store config**. The options are grouped in cards.

  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/01-store-config.png" alt="Store configuration with the Channels, Operation and Locator groups" />

## 2. Change what you need

### Channels

The service types the cashier can start an order with: **Dine in** and **Takeaway**. Tap a card to turn it on or off. **Drive-Thru** can't be turned on yet.

### Operation

**Simultaneous order tabs**: how many orders the cashier can have open at the same time, from 1 to 5. It lets you take a new order while another one waits for payment. The default value is 3.

### Kiosk orders

**Show kiosk orders for**: how long, since it was created, a pending kiosk order keeps showing in **Charge kiosk order**. After that time the customer is no longer waiting at the counter. The options are **No limit**, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or **Other** to type the minutes you need, from 1 to 1440. In Ecuador it comes at 5 minutes; in the other countries, with no limit.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/08-kiosk-orders.png" alt="Kiosk orders group with Show kiosk orders for set to No limit" />
</Frame>

How the cashier sees it: see [Charge a kiosk order in cash](/en/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment).

### Locator

How the customer is called when their order is ready.

- **Ask for a locator at checkout**: shows the locator button in the POS checkout and in the kiosk orders checkout.
- **Locator required**: the POS doesn't let you close the sale without a number or a name. It needs **Ask for a locator at checkout** to be on.
- **Locator type**:
  - **Number**: the cashier enters the number of the buzzer handed to the customer.
  - **Name**: the cashier types the customer's name, as at the kiosk: a single word, letters only, 2 to 12.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/03-locator.png" alt="Locator group with Ask for a locator at checkout, Locator required and Locator type set to Name" />
</Frame>

The number or the name travels in the order in the same field the kiosk uses. By default the locator is on, not required, and set to **Number**.

### Upsell

**Suggest upsell to the cashier**: when the cashier opens a product that the menu has set up with an upsell (for example, sandwich → combo), the POS shows the options with the price difference. It doesn't slow down the sale. It's on in every country.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/05-upsell.png" alt="Upsell group with Suggest upsell to the cashier turned on" />
</Frame>

How the cashier uses it: see [Offer an upsell](/en/manuals/pos/upsell).

### On-screen keyboard

**Turn on for every register in the store**: the POS keyboard shows up when any text field is tapped on every register, as if each cashier turned it on in **Settings**. A register that doesn't need it, for example one with a physical keyboard, turns it off just for itself in **Settings → Hide on this register**, and that choice is kept even if you change this option. It's off by default.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/06-keyboard.png" alt="On-screen keyboard group with Turn on for every register in the store off" />
</Frame>

How the cashier sees it: see [Printer and terminal settings](/en/manuals/pos/terminal-settings).

### Diagnostics

**Order-flow logging** sends the backend a log of what the POS does on each order, so support can investigate a problem. Leave it as it is unless support asks you to change it.

### Billing

- **Billing data required**: when the sale moves to payment, the POS opens **Identify customer** and doesn't let you finish the sale until the cashier saves the details: **Final consumer** or the customer's. It comes off.
- **Final-consumer limit**: above the amount, the POS doesn't let you close the sale as final consumer and requires the customer's document.
- **Limit amount**: write it the way the business says it (`50` is USD 50; in Colombia, `200000` is COP 200,000). The exact amount is still billed as final consumer: the limit applies from the first cent above it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/02-billing-limit.png" alt="Billing group with Billing data required, the final-consumer limit and the amount" />
</Frame>

In Ecuador the limit is on by default at USD 50. In the other countries it's off.

## 3. Save

While there are unsaved changes, **Unsaved** shows at the top, and each option that differs from the country value is marked as **Custom**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/03-unsaved-change.png" alt="Takeaway channel turned off with the Unsaved and Custom marks" />
</Frame>

A bar also appears at the bottom right, **You have unsaved changes**, with **Discard** and **Save**, and it stays in view while you scroll down the page. **Discard** goes back to what was last saved. If you put an option back the way it was, the bar goes away on its own: there is nothing left to save.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/07-save-bar.png" alt="On-screen keyboard turned on but not saved, with the You have unsaved changes bar at the bottom right" />
</Frame>

Tap **Save**. The store's terminals pick up the new configuration without restarting.

<Tip>
  If you already saved and the POS shows the change, you're done.
</Tip>

## Go back to the country defaults

**Restore defaults** returns every option to the default value for the store's country. It asks for confirmation and the changes reach the POS right away.

## Recipes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The store doesn't do takeaway orders">
    1. In **Channels**, tap **Takeaway** to turn it off.
    2. Tap **Save**.
    3. On the POS, that option no longer shows up when starting an order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The store starts handing out buzzers">
    1. In **Locator**, turn on **Ask for a locator at checkout**.
    2. In **Locator type**, choose **Number**.
    3. If no order can go out without a buzzer, also turn on **Locator required**.
    4. Tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The store calls the customer by name">
    1. In **Locator**, turn on **Ask for a locator at checkout**.
    2. In **Locator type**, choose **Name**.
    3. If every order has to go out with a name, turn on **Locator required**.
    4. Tap **Save**. On the POS, the checkout button now says **Name**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Require the customer's details from a certain amount">
    1. In **Billing**, turn on **Final-consumer limit**.
    2. In **Limit amount**, write the amount in the store's currency.
    3. Tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The cashier must always record the billing details">
    1. In **Billing**, turn on **Billing data required**.
    2. Tap **Save**.
    3. On the POS, tapping **Charge** opens **Identify customer**. If the customer doesn't want an invoice in their name, the cashier saves **Final consumer**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Kiosk orders disappear from the register too fast">
    1. In **Kiosk orders**, choose more minutes in **Show kiosk orders for**, type the ones you need in **Other**, or choose **No limit**.
    2. Tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The touch registers need the on-screen keyboard">
    1. In **On-screen keyboard**, turn on **Turn on for every register in the store**.
    2. Tap **Save**.
    3. A register with a physical keyboard turns it off in **Settings → Hide on this register**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A cashier needs to take more orders at once">
    1. In **Operation**, raise **Simultaneous order tabs**.
    2. Tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The store doesn't want upsell suggestions">
    1. In **Upsell**, turn off **Suggest upsell to the cashier**.
    2. Tap **Save**. The POS stops showing **Offer the customer** in the product panel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
  **Writing the limit amount in cents.** The field is in the store's currency: `50` is USD 50. If you write `5000` thinking in cents, the POS only requires the customer's details from USD 5,000.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Turning on Locator required in Number mode with no buzzers in the store.** The POS doesn't let you close any sale without a buzzer number. Turn it on only when the store already hands them out, or switch the type to **Name**.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Restore defaults without checking.** It returns every option to the country value, including the ones the store had adjusted on purpose.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I changed an option and the POS didn't pick it up">
    Confirm that you tapped **Save** and that the **Unsaved** notice went away. Also check that the store in the header is the terminal's store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Custom mean?">
    That the option has a value different from the default value for the store's country.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have a different configuration on each terminal?">
    No: the configuration belongs to the store and applies to all of its terminals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
