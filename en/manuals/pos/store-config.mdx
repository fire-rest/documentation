---
title: "Store configuration"
description: "Adjust how the POS runs in each store: service types, parallel orders, buzzer and when to require the customer's details."
---

Not every store runs the same way: one has a drive-thru and another doesn't, one hands out buzzers and another doesn't, and in some countries the law requires the customer's details above a certain amount. This screen adjusts the POS for each store without touching the terminals: the changes reach them on their own.

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - The configuration is **per store**: it applies to every terminal of the store selected in the header.
  - Each option has a **default value for the store's country**. Whatever you change is marked as **Custom**.
  - Changes reach the POS without restarting it, as soon as you tap **Save**.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Fire backoffice at **[app.fire.rest](https://app.fire.rest)**, with permissions on the store.
- **Channel - POS** and the store selected in the header.

## 1. Open Store config

In the side menu, open **Fire POS → Store config**. The options are grouped in cards.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/01-store-config.png" alt="Store configuration with the Channels, Operation and Buzzer groups" />
</Frame>

## 2. Change what you need

### Channels

The service types the cashier can start an order with: **Dine in** and **Takeaway**. Tap a card to turn it on or off. **Drive-Thru** can't be turned on yet.

### Operation

**Simultaneous order tabs**: how many orders the cashier can have open at the same time, from 1 to 5. It lets you take a new order while another one waits for payment. The default value is 3.

### Buzzer

- **Ask for a buzzer at checkout**: shows the **Buzzer** button in the POS checkout and in the kiosk orders checkout, to record the number of the pager handed to the customer.
- **Buzzer required**: the POS doesn't let you close the sale without a buzzer number. It needs **Ask for a buzzer at checkout** to be on.

### Diagnostics

**Order-flow logging** sends the backend a log of what the POS does on each order, so support can investigate a problem. Leave it as it is unless support asks you to change it.

### Billing

- **Final-consumer limit**: above the amount, the POS doesn't let you close the sale as final consumer and requires the customer's document.
- **Limit amount**: write it the way the business says it (`50` is USD 50; in Colombia, `200000` is COP 200,000). The exact amount is still billed as final consumer: the limit applies from the first cent above it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/02-billing-limit.png" alt="Billing group with the final-consumer limit and the amount" />
</Frame>

In Ecuador the limit is on by default at USD 50. In the other countries it's off.

## 3. Save

While there are unsaved changes, **Unsaved** shows at the top, and each option that differs from the country value is marked as **Custom**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/03-unsaved-change.png" alt="Takeaway channel turned off with the Unsaved and Custom marks" />
</Frame>

Tap **Save**. The store's terminals pick up the new configuration without restarting.

<Tip>
  If you already saved and the POS shows the change, you're done.
</Tip>

---

## Go back to the country defaults

**Restore defaults** returns every option to the default value for the store's country. It asks for confirmation and the changes reach the POS right away.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/store-config/04-reset-defaults.png" alt="Confirmation to restore the country's default values" />
</Frame>

## Recipes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The store doesn't do takeaway orders">
    1. In **Channels**, tap **Takeaway** to turn it off.
    2. Tap **Save**.
    3. On the POS, that option no longer shows up when starting an order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The store starts handing out buzzers">
    1. In **Buzzer**, turn on **Ask for a buzzer at checkout**.
    2. If no order can go out without a buzzer, also turn on **Buzzer required**.
    3. Tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Require the customer's details from a certain amount">
    1. In **Billing**, turn on **Final-consumer limit**.
    2. In **Limit amount**, write the amount in the store's currency.
    3. Tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A cashier needs to take more orders at once">
    1. In **Operation**, raise **Simultaneous order tabs**.
    2. Tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
  **Writing the limit amount in cents.** The field is in the store's currency: `50` is USD 50. If you write `5000` thinking in cents, the POS only requires the customer's details from USD 5,000.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Turning on Buzzer required with no buzzers in the store.** The POS doesn't let you close any sale without a buzzer number. Turn it on only when the store already hands them out.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Restore defaults without checking.** It returns every option to the country value, including the ones the store had adjusted on purpose.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I changed an option and the POS didn't pick it up">
    Confirm that you tapped **Save** and that the **Unsaved** notice went away. Also check that the store in the header is the terminal's store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Custom mean?">
    That the option has a value different from the default value for the store's country.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have a different configuration on each terminal?">
    No: the configuration belongs to the store and applies to all of its terminals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
