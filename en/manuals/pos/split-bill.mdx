---
title: "Split the bill"
description: "Share a bill among several people in even parts, by amount or by what each person had, and charge it part by part."
---

When a table pays separately, working out the bill by hand is where money gets lost: one part charged twice, another that nobody paid. **Split the bill** divides the remaining balance among the people and guides you through charging part by part, until the bill is covered.

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - There are three ways: **Even split**, **By amount** and **By item**.
  - **By item** is only available before the first charge.
  - **Remove split** keeps the charges already made: it only removes the part guide.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- The order on the charge screen. See [Sell and charge](/en/manuals/pos/sell-and-charge).

## 1. Open the split

On the charge screen, tap **Split the bill**. The window divides the remaining balance between two people; with **+** you add more, and with each person's **×** you remove them.

## 2. Choose how to split

- **Even split**: each person pays the same share of the remaining balance.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/manuals/pos/split-bill/01-split-even.png" alt="Splitting the bill evenly between two people" />
  </Frame>

- **By amount**: edit what one person pays and the others adjust so the total adds up.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/manuals/pos/split-bill/02-split-amount.png" alt="Splitting the bill by amount, with each person's amount" />
  </Frame>

- **By item**: on each product, tap the number of the person who had it. Anything you don't assign is charged as **The table**.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/manuals/pos/split-bill/03-split-items.png" alt="Splitting the bill by item, with the products and each person's buttons" />
  </Frame>

At the bottom, the window confirms that the parts add up to the total. Tap **Apply split**.

## 3. Charge each part

The charge screen shows **Bill split** with each part and who you are charging. The amount already comes with that person's share: charge as usual.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/split-bill/04-split-applied.png" alt="Charge screen with the bill split into two parts, none charged" />
</Frame>

When you charge a part, it is marked and the POS moves on to the next one.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/split-bill/05-part-charged.png" alt="Bill split with the first part charged and the second still to charge" />
</Frame>

With all the parts charged, **Account covered** appears: tap **Finish sale**.

<Tip>
  If all the parts are charged and you finished the sale, you're done.
</Tip>

---

## Remove the split

If the table changes its mind, tap **Remove split** and confirm. The charges already made are kept; what's left to charge becomes a single remaining balance again.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/split-bill/06-remove-split.png" alt="Confirmation to remove the split, which keeps the confirmed charges" />
</Frame>

## Recipes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Four friends pay the same">
    1. Tap **Split the bill**.
    2. With **+**, add people until you have four.
    3. Leave **Even split** selected and tap **Apply split**.
    4. Charge each part.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Each person pays for what they ordered">
    1. Before charging anything, tap **Split the bill** and choose **By item**.
    2. On each product, tap the number of the person who ordered it.
    3. Tap **Apply split** and charge each part.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="One person pays a fixed amount and the rest is shared">
    1. Choose **By amount**.
    2. Enter that person's amount: the others adjust on their own.
    3. Tap **Apply split**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
  **Charging by hand without applying the split.** Without the part guide, it's easy to charge one person twice or leave another one uncharged. Apply the split and charge part by part.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It won't let me split by item">
    It's only available before the first charge. If you already charged something, use **Even split** or **By amount** for the remaining balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is “The table”?">
    Whatever you didn't assign to any person when splitting by item. It's charged as one more part.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I removed the split: are the charges lost?">
    No. The confirmed charges are kept; only the part guide is removed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
