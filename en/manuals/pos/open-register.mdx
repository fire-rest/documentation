---
title: "Open the register"
description: "Declare how much cash the drawer starts with so the shift close balances."
---

The money in the drawer at the start of the shift —the fund for giving change— isn't a sale. If it isn't declared, it looks like an overage at close; if it's declared wrong, a shortage shows up that doesn't exist. That's why the POS won't let anyone sell until the cashier declares the initial fund.

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - Each shift opens its own register with an **initial fund**. It can be $ 0.
  - The fund is counted at close: the expected amount is initial fund + cash sales − withdrawals.
  - A cashier can only have **one open register** at a time.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- You've started the shift. See [Start a shift](/en/manuals/pos/start-shift).

## 1. Count the fund

After you start the shift, if the register is closed, the POS shows **Register closed** with the **Initial fund** field.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/open-register/01-register-closed.png" alt="Register closed window with the initial fund at zero, the keypad and the bills and coins pad" />
</Frame>

Declare what's in the drawer in one of two ways:

- **With the bills and coins pad**: tap each bill or coin you count. The total adds up on its own, and below it you see how many pieces you counted.
- **With the keypad**: type the total.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/open-register/02-initial-fund.png" alt="Initial fund of $ 100,000 counted with two $ 50,000 bills" />
</Frame>

## 2. Open the register

Tap **Open register**. The window closes, **Register open** appears at the top, and the POS is ready to sell.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/open-register/03-pos-ready.png" alt="POS sales screen with the register open" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  If you see **Register open** at the top and the product menu, you're done. To sell, see [Sell and charge](/en/manuals/pos/sell-and-charge).
</Tip>

---

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
  **Declaring a fund you didn't count.** If you declare $ 100,000 and there's $ 90,000, the close will show a $ 10,000 shortage under your name. Count the drawer before you open.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Mixing your fund with another shift's.** If the drawer has money from the previous shift, that money belongs to that shift's close. Start with the fund agreed for your shift.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I made a mistake on the pad">
    Tap the **−** next to the bill to remove one, or **Clear** to start over.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I open with $ 0?">
    Yes. If the drawer starts empty, open with $ 0.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says I already have an open register">
    A register of yours was left open on another terminal. Ask the manager to close it from [Terminal administration](/en/manuals/pos/terminal-admin) with **Close register manually**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
