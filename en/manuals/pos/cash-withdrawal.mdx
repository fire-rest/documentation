---
title: "Partial cash withdrawal"
description: "Take cash out of the register in the middle of the shift with the manager's approval, sign the slip, and have the withdrawal deducted at the close."
---

A register that piles up cash is a risk, and sometimes something has to be paid in cash in the middle of the shift. If the cashier takes the money out without recording it, the close shows a shortage nobody can explain. With a **partial withdrawal**, the manager authorizes the amount from the backoffice, the cashier takes it out of the drawer with their PIN and the code, and a slip comes out that both sign. The register close already deducts it.

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - The **manager decides the amount** when generating the code. The cashier does not type it: they confirm it.
  - The code is tied to **one open register**: it only works on that register and for that amount.
  - Confirming opens the drawer and prints a **slip to sign**. The close subtracts the withdrawal from what there should be in the register.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- The register open. See [Open the register](/en/manuals/pos/open-register).
- A manager with access to **Fire POS → Authorization codes** in the backoffice. See [Authorization codes](/en/manuals/pos/authorization-codes).

---

## In the backoffice: authorize the withdrawal

The manager does this.

### 1. Choose the register and the amount

Open **Fire POS → Authorization codes** and, under **Purpose**, choose **Partial withdrawal**. Fill in:

- **Open register**: the register the cash comes out of. If only one is open, it is already selected. The POS shows which one it is in the **For the manager** box.
- **Amount to withdraw**: no thousands separator. Below it you see the amount as the cashier will see it, and it cannot exceed the **Cash in register**.
- **Category**: what the cash is for. See [Categories](#categories).
- **Reason**: optional, except with the **Other** category.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/cash-withdrawal/01-withdrawal-form.png" alt="Partial withdrawal form with the open register selected, the cash in register, the amount, the category and the reason" />
</Frame>

### 2. Generate the code and read it to the cashier

Choose the duration and tap **Generate code**. The code appears in **Codes issued in this session** with the authorized amount. Read the 6 digits to the cashier.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/cash-withdrawal/02-generated-code.png" alt="Withdrawal code generated with the authorized amount and the countdown" />
</Frame>

After generating, the form is cleared for the next code.

## In the POS: make the withdrawal

The cashier does this.

### 1. Open the withdrawal and enter your cashier PIN

Tap your name at the top right to open **Settings** and choose **Withdraw cash**. The POS asks for **your cashier PIN**, the same one you use to sign in to the POS.

The **For the manager** box shows the store and the open register. That is what the manager has to choose in the backoffice so the code works on this register.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/cash-withdrawal/03-pin.png" alt="Cashier PIN step with the cashier's name and the For the manager box with the store and the open register" />
</Frame>

### 2. Enter the manager's code

Enter the 6 digits the manager read to you.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/cash-withdrawal/04-code.png" alt="Manager's code step with the numeric keypad" />
</Frame>

### 3. Check the amount and confirm

The POS shows the **Authorized amount**, the category and the reason. If it is what you agreed, tap **Confirm withdrawal**. The withdrawal is recorded and the drawer opens.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/cash-withdrawal/05-confirm.png" alt="Withdrawal confirmation with the authorized amount, the category and the reason" />
</Frame>

### 4. Take the cash out and declare how much you took

Take the amount out of the drawer and declare it in one of two ways:

- **Amount only**: type it on the numeric keypad.
- **Bill by bill**: tap each bill and coin you take out. The detail goes on the slip.

It has to be exactly what the manager authorized. When it matches, tap **Print slip**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/cash-withdrawal/06-count.png" alt="Step to declare the cash taken out with the amount, the numeric keypad and the bill and coin buttons" />
</Frame>

### 5. Sign the slip

The slip prints and stays on screen, exactly as it came out. The cashier and the manager sign it. If it did not print, tap **Print again**. Tap **Done** to go back to selling.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/cash-withdrawal/07-slip.png" alt="Withdrawal slip on screen with the store, the register, the cashier, who authorized, the category, the amount withdrawn, the bill detail and the signatures" />
</Frame>

The slip carries the store, the register, the cashier, who authorized, the date, the category, the reason, the amount withdrawn, the bill detail if you declared it that way, and both signatures.

<Tip>
  If the slip is signed and filed, you are done.
</Tip>

## How it affects the register close

- **Expected** = opening float + cash sales − withdrawals. The close already subtracts each withdrawal.
- The close summary and its slip show the total of **Withdrawals**.
- Fire's cash reconciliation receives each withdrawal with its amount, its category, the time and who authorized it.

See [Close the register](/en/manuals/pos/register-close).

### Example

A register opens with a $ 100.000 float and sells $ 600.000 in cash. In the afternoon the manager takes $ 300.000 to the safe. At the close there is $ 400.000 in the drawer.

| | Withdrawal recorded in the POS | Cash taken out without recording it |
|---|---|---|
| Opening float | $ 100.000 | $ 100.000 |
| Cash sales | $ 600.000 | $ 600.000 |
| Withdrawals | − $ 300.000 | $ 0 |
| **Expected** | **$ 400.000** | **$ 700.000** |
| Counted at the close | $ 400.000 | $ 400.000 |
| **Difference** | **$ 0: it balances** | **− $ 300.000: shortage** |

With the withdrawal recorded, the close balances and the signed slip explains where the $ 300.000 went. Without recording it, the cashier is left with a shortage for money the manager took.

## Categories

| Category | When to use it |
|----------|----------------|
| **Safe drop** | The cash goes to the store's safe. |
| **Bank deposit** | The cash goes to the bank. |
| **Paid out** | A supplier or a store expense is paid with cash from the register. |
| **Tip out** | Tips are handed out in cash. |
| **Shift handover** | The cash is handed over at a shift change. |
| **Other** | None of the above. It requires a reason. |

## Recipes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Take cash to the safe in the middle of the shift">
    1. The cashier opens **Settings → Withdraw cash** and tells you the register in the **For the manager** box.
    2. In **Fire POS → Authorization codes**, choose **Partial withdrawal**, that register, the amount and **Safe drop**.
    3. Generate the code and read it to the cashier.
    4. The cashier confirms, takes the cash out and declares it.
    5. Sign the slip and keep the cash together with the slip.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pay a supplier with cash from the register">
    1. Generate the code with the **Paid out** category and, in the reason, who is being paid.
    2. The cashier makes the withdrawal and hands the cash to the supplier.
    3. File the signed slip together with the supplier's invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Hand out tips in cash">
    1. Generate the code with the **Tip out** category and the amount to hand out.
    2. The cashier makes the withdrawal and declares the cash bill by bill.
    3. Hand out the cash and file the signed slip.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Withdraw several times during the shift">
    1. Generate one code per withdrawal, with its amount.
    2. The cashier makes each withdrawal separately and signs each slip.
    3. At the close, the summary shows the total of **Withdrawals** and the expected amount already deducts them all.
    4. Check the detail in **Fire POS → Withdrawals**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
  **Generating the code for another register.** The code only works on the register you chose. Before generating it, compare the register with the one in the POS **For the manager** box.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Taking out a different amount than authorized.** The close deducts exactly what you authorized. If more or less cash comes out, the close shows an overage or a shortage.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Not signing or filing the slip.** It is the record of who took the cash out and who authorized it. Without the signatures, a withdrawal cannot be claimed.
</Warning>

## Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Partial withdrawal** | Cash that leaves the register in the middle of the shift, with the manager's approval. It does not close the register. |
| **Authorized amount** | What the manager authorized when generating the code. It is the only amount the cashier can withdraw with that code. |
| **Open register** | A terminal's register session, from when it opens until it closes. The withdrawal code only works on the register the manager chose. |
| **Cash in register** | Opening float + cash sales − withdrawals, as recorded by the POS. It is the withdrawal's ceiling. |
| **Withdrawal slip** | The paper the POS prints at the end of the withdrawal, with the cashier's and the manager's signatures. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It says «That code is for another register»">
    The manager chose another register when generating the code. Ask for a new one for the register in the **For the manager** box.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says «The register doesn't have that much cash»">
    The authorized amount is higher than the cash in the register. The code **was not used**: the manager can generate another one for less.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I do not know which PIN to enter">
    It is your cashier PIN, the same one you use to sign in to the POS. The step title includes your name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The slip did not print">
    The withdrawal is already recorded. Check the printer and tap **Print again**: the slip is still on screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The slip does not have the bill detail">
    It only appears if you declared the cash bill by bill. If you typed the amount, the slip comes out without the detail.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the cashier change the amount?">
    No. The manager decides it when generating the code. If a different amount is needed, generate another code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I make several withdrawals in the same shift?">
    Yes. Each one needs its own code and comes with its own slip.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
