---
title: "Charge a kiosk order in cash"
description: "Charge at the POS the order the customer built on the kiosk and chose to pay in cash, without entering it again."
---

When the customer chooses to **pay in cash** on the kiosk, the order is built but not charged, and the customer goes to the counter. If the cashier enters it again as a new sale, the sale is duplicated and the kiosk order stays pending. **Kiosk** brings that order into the POS so you can charge it as it is.

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - The **Kiosk** button is in the right-hand bar and works with the register open.
  - The list shows the store's kiosk orders **awaiting payment**. In Ecuador, only those from the last **5 minutes**.
  - Charging opens the drawer. If another register charged the same order, the POS warns you so you do not take the cash.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- The kiosk linked and with cash payment enabled. See [Linking](/en/manuals/kiosk/linking).
- An open register in the POS. See [Open the register](/en/manuals/pos/open-register).

## 1. The customer chooses cash on the kiosk

On the kiosk, the customer builds the order and chooses cash at payment. The kiosk tells them to pay at the register.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk-cash-payment/01-kiosk-select-cash.png" alt="Kiosk payment screen with cash selected" />
</Frame>

## 2. Open the kiosk orders

In the POS, tap **Kiosk** in the right-hand bar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment/01-kiosk-button.png" alt="Kiosk button in the POS right-hand bar" />
</Frame>

**Charge kiosk order** opens with the pending orders. Find the customer's order by its code or number in the search box; if they just placed it and it does not appear, tap **Refresh**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment/02-pending-orders.png" alt="Charge kiosk order window with the search box and the Refresh button" />
</Frame>

## 3. Review the order

Tap the order. **Confirm payment** shows the order's short number —for example **61K**—, the code, the **Order detail** and the total. Ask the customer about the order before charging.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment/03-confirm.png" alt="Confirm payment for a kiosk order with the detail, the total and the Buzzer required and Billing details buttons" />
</Frame>

## 4. Assign the buzzer

If the store uses buzzers, tap **Buzzer**, type the number of the buzzer you hand the customer and tap **Save**. If the button says **Buzzer required** in amber, **Charge cash** is not enabled until you enter it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment/04-buzzer.png" alt="Buzzer number window with the number 27 typed in" />
</Frame>

## 5. Enter the billing details

If the customer wants an invoice in their name, tap **Billing details**. Choose the document type, type the number and fill in the details. In Brazil, the CPF and the CNPJ are checked against their check digits. Tap **Save**: the customer's name and document replace **Final consumer** on the order. See [Customer and billing details](/en/manuals/pos/customer-invoice).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment/05-billing-details.png" alt="Identify customer window with CPF, phone, name, address and email" />
</Frame>

## 6. Take the cash and charge

In **Received**, enter the cash the customer hands you with the bills and coins buttons, or type it with the keypad. The POS works out the **Change**. The buzzer and the billing details show as set on the right.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment/06-ready-to-charge.png" alt="Confirm payment with buzzer 27, the billing details entered, R$ 100,00 received and R$ 0,10 change" />
</Frame>

Tap **Charge cash**. The drawer opens so you can put the money away and take out the change. The POS records the charge, prints the receipt and shows **Order paid** with the **Change to return**, the buzzer and the customer.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/kiosk-cash-payment/07-paid.png" alt="Order paid with the buzzer, the customer, the change to return and the charge detail" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  If the order disappeared from the pending list, you are done: it is charged and under your name.
</Tip>

---

## 5-minute window in Ecuador

In Ecuador, the list only shows kiosk orders created in the **last 5 minutes**, and the window says so at the top. In the other countries you see all the day's pending orders.

If the order you chose reaches 5 minutes while you are counting the cash, you can still finish charging it.

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
  **Entering the kiosk order as a new sale.** The sale is duplicated and the kiosk order stays pending. Always charge it from **Kiosk**.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Taking the cash when the POS says another register already charged it.** That order is already paid: if you take the money, the customer pays twice. Give the cash back.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Entering a buzzer that is not the one you handed out.** The buzzer is how the customer is called when their order is ready: with the wrong number, someone else is called. Type the number on the buzzer in the customer's hand.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Charging without asking about the order.** Two kiosk orders can have the same total. Confirm the code or the products with the customer before tapping **Charge cash**.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The order does not appear in the list">
    Tap **Refresh**. Check that the customer confirmed cash payment on the kiosk. In Ecuador, if more than 5 minutes have passed, the order no longer appears: tell the manager.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says “Another register already charged this order”">
    Another terminal charged it while you had it open. Do not take the cash, or give it back to the customer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says “This order is no longer pending”">
    Another register charged it or it was canceled before you opened it. Refresh the list and confirm the code with the customer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says “Another register changed this order”">
    The POS loaded the order's current data. Review it and tap **Charge cash** again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="“Charge cash” is not enabled">
    Check that the amount received covers the total and, if the button says **Buzzer required**, that you entered the buzzer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It will not let me change the billing details">
    The order already has an issued invoice, or it has no customer attached. Charge it with the data it has.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
