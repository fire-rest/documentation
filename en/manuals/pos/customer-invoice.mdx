title: "Customer and billing details"
description: "Enter the customer's document when they ask for an invoice, recover the details of someone who already bought, and what happens when the store requires the details above a certain amount."
---

If you don't identify the customer, the sale goes out to **final consumer**. When the customer asks for an invoice in their name —or the law requires one above a certain amount— you have to enter their document before finishing. A mistyped document ends up on the invoice, and fixing it later costs a credit note.

<Note>
  **The minimum you need to know**

  - With no details, the sale goes out to **Final consumer**.
  - If the customer accepts the terms, their details stay saved: on the next purchase, at the POS or the kiosk, they fill in on their own from the document.
  - If the store has a **Final-consumer limit**, above that amount the POS requires the customer's details.
  - If the store has **Billing data required**, tapping **Charge** opens **Identify customer** and the sale can't be finished until you save it, even as **Final consumer**.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- An order with products. See [Sell and charge](/en/manuals/pos/sell-and-charge).

## 1. Open Identify customer

On the order, tap **Identify customer**. On the charge screen, the same button is called **Billing details**. The window starts on **CONSUMIDOR FINAL**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/customer-invoice/01-customer-dialog.png" alt="Identify customer window with the document type set to final consumer" />
</Frame>

## 2. Choose the type and enter the number

In **Document type**, choose the one the customer shows you. They are the same types and names the kiosk uses; in Colombia: **NIT**, **CEDULA**, **CEDULA DE EXTRANJERIA**, **PASAPORTE** or **CONSUMIDOR FINAL**. The list for each country is further down, in **Document types by country**.

Type the **Document number** with the window's keypad or with the physical keyboard.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/customer-invoice/02-document-number.png" alt="Document number partly typed with the window's keypad" />
</Frame>

If the number doesn't have the right length, the POS warns you when you save and doesn't save it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/customer-invoice/03-document-incomplete.png" alt="Incomplete document number warning when trying to save" />
</Frame>

## 3. If the customer bought before

With the full number, the POS looks up the customer. While it searches, a circle spins inside the field and it says **Looking up customer…**.

If it finds them, a check mark appears with **Customer found** and the empty fields fill in with what they gave on a previous purchase. The filled-in details show in green so you can confirm them with the customer, and the terms checkbox shows as checked with **Accepted in a previous purchase**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/customer-invoice/06-saved-customer.png" alt="Identify customer window with Customer found, the details filled in green and the checkbox checked" />
</Frame>

If the customer gives you a new detail, type over it: the field turns amber with **Undo** to go back to the saved detail, and below the checkbox you see **Saving updates their saved details**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/customer-invoice/07-saved-customer-changed.png" alt="Email field changed in amber with the Undo button and the Saving updates their saved details notice" />
</Frame>

If there are no saved purchases with that document, it says **No saved details** and you carry on as usual.

## 4. Fill in the details and the consent

With a document, the **Name** is required: it's the legal name on the invoice. **Phone**, **Address** and **Email** are optional and say so next to the field name; the invoice is valid without them. If you type an email, it has to be a valid one: the invoice is sent there. The buttons on the right fill in the email domain, such as **@gmail.com**.

If the customer wants their details saved for the next purchase, check **The customer accepts the terms and conditions and to receive promotions**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/customer-invoice/04-customer-filled.png" alt="Identify customer window with the ID, phone, name, address, email and the terms checkbox" />
</Frame>

Tap **Save**. The order shows the customer's name and document.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/pos/customer-invoice/05-customer-identified.png" alt="Order with the customer identified by name and document number" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  If the order already shows the customer's name, you're done: the invoice goes out in their name when you finish the sale and, if they accepted the terms, their details stay saved.
</Tip>

## Document types by country

| Country | Type | Length |
|------|------|-------|
| Colombia | NIT, CEDULA, CEDULA DE EXTRANJERIA | 6 to 10 digits |
| Ecuador | CEDULA | 10 digits |
| Ecuador | RUC | 13 digits |
| Brazil | CPF | 11 digits, with check digits |
| Brazil | CNPJ | 14 digits, with check digits |
| Argentina | CEDULA, DNI | Up to 8 digits |
| Argentina | CUIL, CUIT | Up to 11 digits |
| Venezuela | CEDULA | 6 to 8 digits |
| Venezuela | RIF | 10 to 12 digits |
| All | PASAPORTE (PASSAPORTE in Brazil) | Up to 50 characters, with letters |

The other types —the NIT in Argentina, the RUT and the CEDULA in Chile, and V, E, F and A in Venezuela— accept up to 20 characters. The RUT accepts the **K** check digit.

## How a customer is saved

- It's saved after the sale is finished, only if the terms checkbox was checked. The sale doesn't wait for it: if the system doesn't respond, the sale goes through anyway.
- A customer is their document type and number: the same ID number as **CEDULA DE EXTRANJERIA** is another customer.
- **Final consumer** is never saved.

Saved customers are viewed and deleted in the backoffice: see [Fire customers](/en/manuals/backoffice/customers).

## Final-consumer limit

If the store has the **Final-consumer limit** turned on, sales above that amount can't be closed as final consumer: the charge screen shows **Invoice required** and you have to enter the customer's details to finish. The limit is set in the backoffice: see [Store configuration](/en/manuals/pos/store-config). In Ecuador it comes turned on at USD 50.

## Billing data required

If the store has **Billing data required** turned on, the cashier has to record the billing details on every sale. Tapping **Charge** opens **Identify customer** by itself. If the customer doesn't want an invoice in their name, tap **Save** with **Final consumer**.

If you close the window without saving, the button on the charge screen changes to **Billing details missing**, **Finish sale** is blocked and a notice with **Record billing details** shows up above it, which opens the window again. It's turned on in the backoffice: see [Store configuration](/en/manuals/pos/store-config).

## Change or remove the customer

Tap the customer's name on the order to edit their details. **Remove current customer** sets the sale back to final consumer.

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
  **Typing the document without reading it back.** The POS checks the length, not that the document belongs to the customer: one wrong digit ends up on the invoice. Read the number back to the customer before you save.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Confirming the filled-in details without asking.** What comes from a previous purchase may be out of date: if the customer changed email or address, the invoice goes out with the old detail. Ask them before you save.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Entering the details after finishing.** Once the sale is finished, the invoice has already gone out to final consumer. Identify the customer before you tap **Finish sale**.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It says “The document number is incomplete”">
    The number has fewer digits than that document type requires. Check the type and the number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer already bought and it says “No saved details”">
    On that purchase they didn't accept the terms, used another document type or bought in another country. Enter their details and check the checkbox: next time they fill in on their own.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are the phone, address or email needed?">
    No. The invoice only needs the document type and number and the name. Ask for the email if the customer wants to get the invoice by email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Identify customer opened when I tapped Charge">
    The store asks for the billing details on every sale. If the customer doesn't want an invoice in their name, tap **Save** with **Final consumer**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="“Invoice required” appears on the charge screen">
    The sale is above the store's final-consumer limit. Enter the customer's details so you can finish.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
