You have the [menu](/en/manuals/backoffice/menus) built and the [price list](/en/manuals/backoffice/price-lists) ready. None of that sells yet: what's missing is saying **which store, which channel and which fulfillment type** they're used for. That decision is this screen, and it's also where you press the button that pushes the menu to the channel.

Go to **Restaurant OS → Menu and Products → Menu assignment**.

  <img src="/images/manuals/backoffice/menu-assignments/01-matriz.png" alt="Menu assignment matrix by store, channel and fulfillment" />
</Frame>

## The minimum you need to know

<Note>
**1. The unit is the row: store × channel × fulfillment.** One row per possible combination. The same store appears several times, once per channel and fulfillment type it serves.

**2. A row needs a menu *and* a price list.** With only one of them it can't be synced: the button stays disabled.

**3. Assigning does not publish. Publishing is Sync.** And **Sync** sends what exists **at that moment**, not what existed when you assigned.
</Note>

---

## The simple path

You opened a new store and it needs to start selling:

1. Filter by that store with the **Store** selector.
2. On each row you care about, **Assign menu**.
3. On the same row, **Assign list**.
4. Select the rows and **Sync**.

The status turns **Pending**, and when the channel confirms it settles on **Synced**. That's it.

<Tip>
If your operation is one store and one channel, you're done. The rest of the manual is for when it's forty stores and they all have to change on the same day.
</Tip>

## The matrix: one row per combination

Each row is a real selling destination. These are its columns:

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Store** | The store, with its code underneath. |
| **Group** | Which store groups it belongs to, as colored chips. |
| **Channel** | Web, app, kiosk, aggregator… |
| **Fulfillment** | Delivery, pickup, dine-in… |
| **Menu** | Which menu it uses, marked **Golden** or **Custom** underneath. If it has none, an **Assign menu** button. |
| **Price list** | Which list it's priced with. If it has none, **Assign list**. |
| **Sync** | **No record** · **Pending** · **Synced** · **Failed**, plus the warnings if there were any. |
| **Generated** | When the menu was last built for this destination — even if that send then failed. |

At the top there are seven filters — store, group, channel, fulfillment, menu, list and sync status — and a search that matches store, menu and channel names. With forty stores and five channels the matrix has two hundred rows: the filters are not decoration.

<Info>
**What store groups are for.** A group lets you filter every store in a region, a brand or a format at once, and assign them the same menu in a single operation. Without groups, changing a region's delivery menu means ticking stores by hand and missing one.
</Info>

## Assigning in bulk

Tick several rows with the checkboxes and the bulk action bar appears: **Assign menu**, **Assign list** and **Sync**, with the count of what's selected.

The dialog always states how many combinations the change will apply to before you confirm.

**Any menu can be assigned to any row.** The menu is the structure; the fulfillment type comes from the row. The Golden and your custom menus all appear in the list, each marked **Golden** or **Custom**, whatever fulfillment types you selected — you can mix Delivery, Takeaway and Dine-In rows in the same operation.

<Note>
The assign-menu dialog still carries an old description saying only menus of the row's fulfillment type are listed. Ignore it: every menu is listed.
</Note>

It also works the other way round: **Remove assignment** takes the menu or the list off the selected rows. A row left without a menu or without a list can no longer be synced, goes back to **No record** and loses its preview. What the channel already received stays there.

You can select **up to 20 rows at a time**, for assigning and for syncing. When you reach the limit, the screen warns you: *"You can select up to 20 rows at a time: a larger batch does not finish and leaves the vendor locked."* There's no "select everything matching the filter": with more rows than that, work page by page.

## Sync: what actually happens

**Sync** does three things, in this order:

1. **It flattens the menu.** It takes the menu's structure, applies the assigned list's prices and that row's fulfillment type, and builds a flat menu with no inheritance and no formulas: the literal list of what is sold and for how much.
2. **It sends it to the channel.**
3. **It records the answer.** Channels connected through X-MART, such as the POS and the Kiosk, answer later, on their own time: the row sits on **Pending** (*"Sync dispatched — awaiting X-MART confirmation"*) and then settles on **Synced** or **Failed**. Aggregator channels answer on the spot, so the row goes straight to **Synced** or **Failed**.

The sync also respects what the day's operation turned off in [Out of stock](/en/manuals/backoffice/out-of-stock): those products travel as unavailable. If Fire can't read those turn-offs at that moment, the sync is cancelled rather than sent without them — otherwise everything turned off would come back on in the channel.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/backoffice/menu-assignments/04-estados.png" alt="The four sync states in the Sync column" />
</Frame>

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **No record** | This combination was never synced, or it lost its menu or list. |
| **Pending** | It was sent and the channel hasn't answered yet. |
| **Synced** | The channel received and accepted it. |
| **Failed** | The channel rejected it, or it couldn't be sent — for example, the menu is archived or no product had a price in the list. Hover over the chip to read the reason. |

While there are rows in **Pending**, the screen **refreshes itself every fifteen seconds**. No need to reload or sit watching: you can walk away and come back.

The button stays disabled — *"Assign a menu and a price list first"* — while the row is missing the menu or the list. That's on purpose: without a list, every price would go out at zero.

---

## The snapshot is from now, not from yesterday

<Info>
**There are no versions. Sync sends the current state.** It takes the menu and the list exactly as they are saved in that second. There is no "publish Monday's version" and no going back to an earlier snapshot.

That has a practical consequence worth keeping in mind: **if somebody left the menu half-edited, that's what goes out.** Before syncing in bulk, it's worth confirming the menu is in the state it should be.
</Info>

The reverse is also true, and that's the comfortable part: you don't need to "republish the menu" after changing a price. That row's next sync already carries the new price, because it reads the list at that moment.

## Sale prices travel with the sync

A sync carries the sales of the row's price list, not only the regular prices.

- **A product on sale** goes out with its regular price and its sale price, and the channel shows it on sale.
- **A combo** goes out with its **"from" price** — what the cheapest configuration costs with the sales applied — and, as the struck price, the same configuration without sales. If you typed a suggested price for the combo, that one is the struck price.
- **Inside a combo, an option inherits the standalone product's sale**, unless it has its own price in that combo. With its own price, the combo's price (and its own sale, if it has one) is what counts.

This is what the Kiosk shows, measured in staging on the **Combo para 2** (store 1350, Kiosk, Takeaway and Dine-In):

| On screen | What it is | Combo para 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Header price** | The cheapest configuration, with sales | R$ 67.90 |
| **Struck price** | The same configuration without sales | R$ 72.90 |
| **Badge** | Computed by the Kiosk from the two prices | -7 % |
| **Each option of a required question** | What it **adds** over the cheapest option of its question | 6 Tiras: R$ 20.00 |
| **Each option of an optional question** | Its full add-on price | Batata Média: R$ 10.90 |

The struck price and the badge describe only the cheapest configuration: a customer who picks options that aren't on sale pays the regular amount.

<Warning>
**A sale on one product can change the "from" price of many combos at the next sync.** Every combo where that product is an option without its own price inherits the sale. In staging, putting Coca Cola 400 ml on sale moved about seventy combos at once. It also changes what the other options of that question show as their extra, because the product on sale becomes the cheapest one.
</Warning>

How sales are set up is in [Price lists](/en/manuals/backoffice/price-lists).

## It synced, but something was left out

A row can end up **Synced** and still have dropped things along the way. When that happens, a warnings chip appears in the **Sync** column; clicking it opens the list.

| Warning | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **Product with no price in the assigned list** | The product **was not sent**. Had it been sent, it would have been published at **0**. |
| **Upselling suggestions not in this menu** | The product suggests an upselling item that isn't on this menu. The suggestion doesn't travel. |
| **Cross-selling suggestions not in this menu** | The same, for cross-selling suggestions. |
| **Combo with no questions in this destination** | For this row's fulfillment type the combo has no question left, so it goes out with a "from" price of **0**. |
| **Unresolved modifier groups** | A modifier group couldn't be built and was left out of the menu. |
| **The list also has its own price for this fulfillment type** | Nothing was dropped: the list has a separate price for this row's fulfillment type on some products, and the warning says how many. **That** price is the one published, not the base price you see in the list. |

The last one can appear as soon as you assign the list, before any sync: it warns you at the moment of the decision.

<Warning>
**The first one is the one that costs sales in silence.** The sync says **Synced**, the menu reached the channel, and that product is simply not on the menu the customer sees. Nobody gets an error: you have to open the warnings to find out.

It's fixed by giving the product a price in the list assigned to that row and syncing again. If **no** product has a price, nothing is sent and the row ends **Failed**.
</Warning>

## Batches: 20 at a time, one at a time

You can select **up to 20 rows** per operation. Building and sending each menu takes several seconds, and a bigger batch doesn't finish in time: a batch cut halfway leaves the vendor locked until someone frees it by hand. That's why the limit is in the selection itself — you can't put together a batch that is known not to finish.

On top of that, **only one batch per vendor can be in flight**. If an earlier batch is still waiting for the channel's confirmation, the new **Sync** is refused with a message saying a sync is in progress. Wait for those rows to leave **Pending** and try again.

<Tip>
If you're going to sync many rows, do it in tidy batches — by region, by channel — instead of jumping across pages. When something fails you'll know exactly which group to check.
</Tip>

Selected rows missing a menu or a list **are skipped automatically**, and the screen tells you how many.

## Seeing exactly what was sent

The eye button, **View sync preview**, opens that row's last snapshot: the menu exactly as it was last built and sent — or attempted, if the send failed. To know whether it reached the channel, look at the **Sync** status, not the preview.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/backoffice/menu-assignments/06-preview.png" alt="Preview of the snapshot sent to the channel" />
</Frame>

It has three tabs:

- **Menu** — the menu as it ended up: categories, products, prices and modifiers.
- **Snapshot JSON** — the same menu, raw.
- **Sync payload JSON** — exactly what was sent, or attempted, to the channel.

The top line says when it was last flattened. If the row was never synced, the button is disabled: *"No snapshot generated yet. Sync this row to create one."*

For an argument with a channel — *"this never reached me"* — the last two tabs are the evidence. That same last-built menu is also what partners connected to Fire with an API key read for that store.

## Changing the price for a single destination

There's a separate screen, **Prices and availability**, for adjusting prices and visibility on **one specific destination** without opening the whole menu or the whole list. It works on the rows that already have a menu and a list assigned from here.

Its rule is worth knowing, because it isn't obvious:

- The **price** you change there lives in **the list**, and that list may be shared by several destinations.
- **Visibility** lives in **the menu**, which may also be in use across several destinations.
- Saving sends a quick price and availability update **only to that destination**, and only if it's an aggregator channel. The other destinations pick the change up on their next Sync.

In other words: the change is already saved for everyone, but at most one destination received it. The screen shows a banner before you save with how many destinations share the list and the menu.

<Warning>
**On destinations connected through X-MART — POS and Kiosk — saving there sends nothing.** The price is saved, but the channel only gets it when you **Sync** that row from Menu assignment.
</Warning>

## Recipes: how the real cases get solved

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Putting a new store on sale">
    1. Filter by the store with the **Store** selector. Its rows will appear, one per channel and fulfillment type.
    2. Select all the ones that store will serve (up to 20 at a time).
    3. **Assign list** with the list that belongs to it.
    4. **Assign menu** with its menu. Mixing fulfillment types in the selection is fine.
    5. Select everything again and **Sync**.
    6. Wait for the statuses to go from **Pending** to **Synced**, and check whether any row came back with warnings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Changing the delivery menu across a whole region">
    1. Filter by **Group** with that region's group, and by **Fulfillment** on delivery.
    2. Select the rows, up to 20 per page.
    3. **Assign menu** with the new menu. The dialog confirms how many combinations it applies to.
    4. **Sync**. If there are more rows, move to the next page and repeat once the previous batch has left **Pending**.

    The price list is untouched: changing the menu doesn't change what things cost.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stopping sales on a channel">
    1. Filter by that **Channel**.
    2. Select the rows.
    3. **Assign menu → Remove assignment**.

    The rows are left without a menu and can no longer be synced. Careful: **this does not delete from the channel what was already sent** — that gets turned off on the channel. What you achieve is that no future change reaches it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You raised prices and want them to reach the channel">
    After saving the increase in the [price list](/en/manuals/backoffice/price-lists):

    1. Filter by **Price list** with the list you touched. Every row using it comes up.
    2. Select them, up to 20 at a time.
    3. **Sync**, page by page.

    No menu needs to be opened: the flattening reads the list at that moment and carries the new prices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Putting a product on sale">
    1. Set the sale price in the [price list](/en/manuals/backoffice/price-lists) of the destinations that should have it.
    2. Filter by that **Price list** and **Sync** its rows, up to 20 at a time.
    3. Open **View sync preview** on one row to confirm the product carries its sale.
    4. Remember the combos: every combo where that product is an option without its own price now shows a lower "from" price too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A product isn't showing in the app and nobody knows why">
    Walk it in this order, which goes from the most common to the rarest:

    1. **Is the row Synced?** If it's **Failed**, that's where the trip ended — hover over the chip to read why.
    2. **Are there warnings?** If the product has no price in the assigned list, it wasn't sent — that's the most frequent cause.
    3. **Open View sync preview → Menu tab.** If the product isn't there, it never left Fire.
    4. **Open the assigned [menu](/en/manuals/backoffice/menus).** Check whether it's removed (**Removed**) or hidden (**Hidden**).
    5. **Check [Out of stock](/en/manuals/backoffice/out-of-stock).** It may be turned off by the day's operation.

    If it does appear in the preview and still isn't visible in the app, the problem is no longer in Fire.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## A price change, followed end to end

You raised a product from **10.90** to **11.99** on the *Delivery* list. This is what happens at each link:

| Where | What happens | Anything to do? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price list** | You save 11.99 | Done |
| **Menu** | It doesn't find out, and doesn't need to: the menu stores no prices | Nothing |
| **Assignment** | The rows using that list still show **Synced**, with the old price published | **Yes: sync** |
| **Sync** | Flattens the menu with the list *as it is now* → 11.99 | — |
| **Channel** | Confirms, the row settles on **Synced**, and the customer sees 11.99 | — |

The row to watch is the third one: **a Synced status doesn't mean what's published is current**, it means the last sync went through. If you changed prices after that dispatch, the channel is still on the old ones until the next.

## Mistakes that cost money

<Warning>
**Changing the menu or the list and not syncing.** The assignment is saved and the channel keeps selling with the previous one. **Synced** refers to the last dispatch, not to what's in the menu today.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Ignoring the warnings chip.** The sync goes through and products still never reached the channel. The most expensive one is the product with no price in the assigned list: it drops out of the dispatch without anyone getting an error.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Filtering 60 rows and assuming they all went.** Only 20 can be selected at a time. If you synced the first page and moved on, the other forty stores are still on the old menu.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Syncing with the menu half-edited.** Sync sends what is saved at that moment, with no versions. A menu somebody left half done gets published half done.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Putting a product on sale without thinking about the combos.** The sale also lowers the "from" price of every combo where that product is an option without its own price, on the next sync of each row.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Editing prices in Prices and availability and assuming the Kiosk or POS got them.** On those channels saving sends nothing: the row has to be synced from here.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Removing the assignment thinking it pulls the menu off the channel.** Removing it stops new changes from reaching it, but the last thing sent stays published on the channel's side.
</Warning>

## Glossary

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Row** *(store × channel × fulfillment)* | The combination that is the unit of everything that happens here. |
| **Assign** | Saying which menu and which list a row uses. It publishes nothing. |
| **Sync** | Flattening the menu with its list and sending it to the channel. This one does publish. |
| **Snapshot** | The flat menu that comes out of crossing the menu with the list, with no inheritance and no formulas. It's the last one built, whether or not the send succeeded. |
| **Sync payload** | What was actually sent, or attempted, to the channel. Visible in the preview. |
| **Warning** | Something left out of the dispatch, or worth knowing about it, without making it fail. |
| **"From" price** | A combo's header price: what its cheapest configuration costs, with sales applied. |
| **Struck price** | The price shown crossed out next to a sale: the regular price, or the suggested price you typed for a combo. |
| **Store group** | A label that groups stores so they can be filtered and assigned in bulk. |
| **Pending** | It was sent and the channel hasn't confirmed yet. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I assigned the menu but nothing changed in the app">
    The sync is missing. Assigning only records which menu that row uses; the channel finds out when somebody presses **Sync**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Sync button is greyed out">
    The row is missing the menu or the price list. Both are needed: without a list, every price would be published at zero. The tooltip says it: *"Assign a menu and a price list first"*.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't tick more rows">
    You reached the 20-row limit. Deselect one to pick another, or finish this batch and move on to the next page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sync was refused because a sync is in progress">
    An earlier batch from the same vendor is still waiting for the channel's confirmation. When those rows leave **Pending**, sync again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The row ended up Failed">
    Hover over the **Failed** chip to read the reason. The usual ones: the channel rejected the dispatch, the menu is archived, or no product had a price in the list. Fix it and sync that row on its own to see whether the error repeats. On the menu you'll also see the *"Sync failed — menu snapshot is outdated"* banner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My combos' price changed and I didn't touch them">
    Most likely one of their options went on sale. A combo inherits the sale of every option that has no price of its own in that combo, and its "from" price drops at the next sync. Check the price list of that row.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I republish last week's version?">
    No. There is no versioning: every Sync flattens the menu and the list exactly as they are at that moment. To go back, you undo the change on the menu or the list and sync again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
