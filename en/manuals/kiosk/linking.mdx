---
title: "Linking"
description: "How to link a kiosk to a store from the Fire backoffice."
---

This guide explains how to register a restaurant and link a kiosk device to Fire so it can receive its configuration, menu and payment methods.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Fire backoffice at **[app.fire.rest](https://app.fire.rest)**, with permissions on the **Kiosks** channel.
- The **FIRE POS** (kiosk) app installed on the device.
- The device powered on and connected to the internet.

## 1. Select country and vendor

From the top-right corner, open the country selector and choose the country and vendor you will operate under.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/01-country.png" alt="Country and vendor selector in the backoffice" />
</Frame>

## 2. Confirm the Kiosks channel

At the top of the left side menu (the first option), open the channel selector and choose **Canal - Kioscos** (Kiosks channel). Every action in this guide happens inside that channel.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/02-channel.png" alt="Channel selector with Kiosks channel selected" />
</Frame>

## 3. Register a restaurant

Before linking a device you need a restaurant to attach it to. If the restaurant already exists, skip to step 4.

1. In the side menu open **Devices → Restaurants**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/03-devices-restaurants.png" alt="Devices submenu with the Restaurants option" />
   </Frame>

2. Click **+ New Restaurant**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/04-add-restaurant.png" alt="Restaurants screen with the New Restaurant button" />
   </Frame>

3. **Step 1 of 2 — Restaurant Information.** Fill in the fields and click **Next**.

   - **City**
   - **Restaurant** (selecting it auto-fills the name and external code)
   - **Administrator Password**

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/05-modal-restaurant-1.png" alt="Step 1 of the New Restaurant modal" />
   </Frame>

4. **Step 2 of 2 — Payment Methods.** Select and configure the payment methods for this restaurant (Cash, Posnet, MercadoPago). Click **Save**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/06-modal-restaurant-2.png" alt="Step 2 of the modal with payment methods" />
   </Frame>

## 4. Open the kiosk app

In parallel, open the **FIRE POS** app on the device. The home screen shows a QR code and the kiosk's **Device ID**. Keep this screen visible.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/08-kiosk-qr.png" alt="Kiosk screen showing the QR code and Device ID" />
</Frame>

## 5. Register the kiosk in the backoffice

1. In the side menu open **Devices → All devices**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/07-devices-tab.png" alt="Devices submenu with the All devices option" />
   </Frame>

2. Click **+ New Kiosk**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/09-add-device.png" alt="Kiosks screen with the New Kiosk button" />
   </Frame>

3. **Step 1 of 2 — Kiosk Information.** Fill in the fields and click **Next**.

   - **Device ID**: copy or scan it from the kiosk screen (step 4).
   - **Kiosk Name**: an internal name to identify it.
   - **Restaurant Template**: the restaurant created in step 3. Its data is auto-filled.
   - **Administrator Password**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/10-device-form-1.png" alt="Step 1 of the New Kiosk modal" />
   </Frame>

4. **Step 2 of 2 — Payment Methods.** Configure the payment methods specific to this kiosk and click **Save**.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/11-device-form-2.png" alt="Step 2 of the New Kiosk modal with payment methods" />
   </Frame>

5. The kiosk now appears in the list with its name, device ID, restaurant and configured payment methods.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/12-device-form-3.png" alt="Kiosks list showing the new device" />
   </Frame>

## 6. The kiosk configures itself

Once saved, the device receives the configuration without manual intervention. The progress is shown on screen:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/13-kiosk-configuring.png" alt="Kiosk screen showing the automatic setup in progress" />
</Frame>

When it finishes, the kiosk shows the confirmation. Tap **Accept** to start using it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/manuals/kiosk/14-kiosk-ready.png" alt="Kiosk screen with setup completed" />
</Frame>

<Check>
  The kiosk is now linked to the store and ready to operate.
</Check>
